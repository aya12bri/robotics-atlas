Every robot is a chain of joints

Each tag is one joint. A joint turns (R) or slides (P): its value is an angle or a distance. Six joints are enough to put a tool anywhere, at any angle.

The highlighted joint moves, the others wait.

02 / 04

Frames, then one table

Attach a frame to every joint: z along the joint axis, x along the common perpendicular. Four numbers per joint describe the whole robot. This is the DH table, and the angles in it are live.

Say where, the robot finds how

Move your pointer. The blue marker is the target, and the robot solves its inverse kinematics on every frame to put the tool on it.

Pick, carry, place

Four poses computed by the same solver: above the cube, on the cube, above the second pad, and on it. The robot pages let you take control.

Meet the family

Eight kinds of robots, from a simple arm to a single wheel. Use the arrows, the dots or your keyboard to move from one to the next.

1 / 8

3-DOF robot arm

R-R-R3 degrees of freedom, serial arm

The simplest arm that can reach a point in space. The best place to learn frames, DH tables and the Jacobian.

Used in
Teaching, light pick and place, small lab automation.
End effectors
Pinch gripper, suction cup, pen or light tool.
Open the 3D model

2 / 8

Cartesian gantry

P-P-P3 degrees of freedom, three sliding axes

Three sliding axes at right angles (x, y, z), like a 3D printer or a CNC machine.

Used in
CNC machines, 3D printers, warehouse picking, large pick and place, laboratory handling.
End effectors
Pinch gripper, suction cup, spindle, print head, camera.
Open the 3D model

3 / 8

SCARA

R-R-P-R4 degrees of freedom, serial arm

Stiff in the vertical direction, flexible in the horizontal plane. Fast and precise assembly from above.

Used in
Electronics assembly, screwing, dispensing, packaging, fast pick and place.
End effectors
Pinch gripper, vacuum nozzle, screwdriver, glue dispenser.
Open the 3D model and the pick-and-place demo

4 / 8

6-axis industrial robot

6 × R6 degrees of freedom, serial arm

The classic robot of the car factory: any position, any orientation, thanks to its spherical wrist.

Used in
Welding, painting, machine tending, palletizing, assembly.
End effectors
Gripper, vacuum, welding torch, paint gun, tool changer.
Open the 3D model and the pick-and-place demo

5 / 8

Collaborative robot (UR5)

6 × R6 degrees of freedom, works next to people

Real UR5 numbers, an offset wrist and force limiting: a robot that can work without a fence.

Used in
Small batches, screwdriving, packing, inspection, laboratories.
End effectors
Electric pinch gripper, soft gripper, screwdriver, camera.
Open the 3D model and the pick-and-place demo

6 / 8

Delta robot

3 × Rparallel robot, 3 arms and 1 optional rotation

Three light arms and parallelograms hold a small platform: extremely fast, but a small workspace.

Used in
Food, pharma and electronics packing on conveyors, more than 100 picks per minute.
End effectors
Vacuum cups, soft fingers, pinch gripper.
Open the 3D model and the pick-and-place demo

7 / 8

Mobile robot (differential drive)

2 wheelsmobile robot, x, y and heading

A robot that moves around instead of reaching: wheel speeds, turning circle, odometry and programs.

Used in
Warehouses, hospitals, factories, cleaning, delivery.
End effectors
Usually none (it carries things); a mobile manipulator adds an arm.
Open the 3D model and the programs

Fixed, castor, omni, Mecanum and spherical wheels; differential, Ackermann, 3-omni and Mecanum drives; sensors and navigation.
