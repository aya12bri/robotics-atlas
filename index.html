Articulated arm (6 axes)

Tasks: welding, painting, palletizing, assembly.

Joints: 6 rotating joints.

SCARA

Tasks: fast pick-and-place, electronics assembly.

Joints: 2 rotating, 1 sliding, 1 rotating.

Delta

Tasks: sorting and packing food at high speed.

Joints: 3 arms working in parallel.

Mobile robot

Tasks: carrying goods in warehouses, cleaning.

Joints: two driven wheels.
