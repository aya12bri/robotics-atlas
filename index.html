Articulated arm (6 axes)

Tasks: welding, painting, palletizing, assembly.

Joints: 6 rotating joints.

Articulated arm (6 axes)

Joints: 6 rotating joints (R-R-R-R-R-R).

Used in: car factories (welding, painting), palletizing, machine tending, assembly, material handling.

End effectors: yes, a pinch gripper (pince) works; also welding torch, spot-welding gun, vacuum suction cup, paint gun, screwdriver. With 6 axes it can pick an object from any direction.

Collaborative robot (cobot, UR-style)

Joints: 6 rotating joints, force-limited so it can work next to people.

Used in: screwing, packaging, lab automation, machine tending, quality inspection in small factories.

End effectors: yes, a pinch gripper (pince) is the most common; also suction cups, screwdrivers, cameras.

SCARA

Joints: rotating, rotating, sliding, rotating (R-R-P-R). → 3D model

Used in: electronics assembly, screwing, fast pick-and-place, packaging, dispensing glue.

End effectors: yes, a pinch gripper works; most often a vacuum suction cup, also a screwdriver or a dispensing nozzle. The tool always points down.

Delta (parallel robot)

Joints: 3 arms working in parallel, all motors fixed on top.

Used in: food and pharma packaging, sorting objects on a conveyor belt with a camera, at over 100 picks per minute.

End effectors: mostly vacuum suction cups or soft grippers. Small pinch grippers are possible, but the payload is light (about 1 to 8 kg at most).

Cartesian robot (gantry)

Joints: 3 sliding joints at right angles (P-P-P). → 3D model

Used in: 3D printers, CNC machines, laboratory pipetting, warehouse picking, palletizing, gluing.

End effectors: very flexible: pinch gripper, suction cup, 3D-printing nozzle, cutting spindle, pipette, laser.

Mobile robot (AMR / AGV)

Joints: two driven wheels (differential drive) plus a free caster wheel.

Used in: warehouses (carrying shelves and boxes), hospitals, cleaning, inspection and security.

End effectors: usually none. It carries a load on top, a fork or a tow hook. It can also carry a robot arm with a gripper, which is then called a mobile manipulator.
