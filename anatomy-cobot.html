What is inside each joint?

  • Compact joint modules: each joint is a tube with a brushless motor, a harmonic-drive gearbox and the electronics inside.
  • Two encoders per joint (motor side and output side) and a torque sensor or current sensing: the controller feels contact forces and stops when it touches a person.
  • Brake in every joint, and a safety-rated stop circuit.
  • Cables run inside the tubes, with the controller in a separate box connected by one cable.
  • Rounded shapes, light weight, limited force: made to work next to people (see “How a cobot stays safe”).
