Definition

A tracked robot moves on two tracks, like a small tank. Each track is driven by its own motor, and the robot turns by running one track faster than the other. Tracks spread the weight and grip rough ground: mud, sand, stairs, rubble. Used for rescue, bomb disposal and inspection.

Track speeds

Set the speed of each track. The same speed: it goes straight. Different speeds: it turns. Opposite speeds: it turns on the spot.

A rubber belt with grousers (ridges) that goes round the wheels. It spreads the weight over a large area: the robot does not sink in mud or sand, and it climbs over steps and stones.

At the back, a toothed wheel (the sprocket) pulls the track. It is turned by a motor and a gearbox (seen in X-ray); the front wheel (the idler) only keeps the track tight.

Small wheels under the robot press the track on the ground, so that the weight is spread along the whole length of the track.

A lidar that turns on top to map the place, a stereo camera at the front to see the obstacles, and headlights for the dark.

A large battery in the body, low down to keep the robot stable on slopes, and a computer that drives the tracks and builds the map. Seen in X-ray.

Sends the video to the operator and receives the orders: rescue and inspection robots are often driven from far away, out of danger.

What is inside each joint?

  • Two motors, one per track, each with a gearbox and an encoder: like a differential-drive robot, but the tracks slip when it turns, so the odometry drifts more.
  • Rough ground: low pressure on the ground, stairs and rubble; some robots add flippers at the front to climb higher obstacles.
  • Uses: rescue, bomb disposal, inspection of tunnels and nuclear plants, agriculture and construction.

The tracked robot really drives: each track has its own motor, and the robot turns by running one track faster than the other (skid steering). The tracks have to slide sideways to turn, so it turns less than a robot on two wheels and uses more energy; that is the price for crossing rough ground. The model is simplified.
